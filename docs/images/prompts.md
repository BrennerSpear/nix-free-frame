Clearly distinguish micro-USB-B from USB-C: micro B has asymmetric shallow trapezoid form, not an oval; do not substitute a USB-A tip for the close-up. Small footer exactly “Schematic only. Port and screw placement vary by revision; follow the linked teardown.” No code, no actual device photos, no personal images, no technical dimensions, no logos, no decorative side rules. Clear usable diagram with minimal text and generous space.
